# Problem 7: Rank Targets and Emails

## Reflection

The agent did a good job selecting companies that align with Open Capital's work. I would say that ThriveAgric is the best match, followed by Bopinc, and lastly AgroMall. This is largely based on what I think the size of the organizations is and whether or not they would be able to pay for consulting services from Open Capital.

I want to note, though, that there was no pricing data that the agent could find, so there was no way for it to determine alignment on organizational size and ability to pay.

The emails are not as tailored as I would have liked. I would have liked them to reference specific work Open Capital has done in relation to potential challenges these organizations might need support with. The emails are a bit generic, and I also do not like that they explain what the organizations are doing back to them as though they do not already know. I would have liked the emails to say more about what Open Capital can do and include links, where possible, to similar work.

## Target Ranking

1. ThriveAgric
2. Bopinc
3. AgroMall

## Main Takeaways

- The selected targets generally align with Open Capital's work.
- ThriveAgric appears to be the strongest match, followed by Bopinc and AgroMall.
- The ranking is an informed judgment because public pricing and financial information were unavailable.
- The outreach emails need more company-specific research and stronger references to Open Capital's relevant work.
- Future drafts should focus less on explaining each recipient's own business and more on the specific value Open Capital can provide.
- Future drafts should include links to relevant Open Capital case studies, projects, or published work when available.
